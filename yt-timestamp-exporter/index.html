Frequently asked questions

Does this work in YouTube Studio?

Yes. The hotkey and capture overlay work both on standard youtube.com watch pages and on YouTube Studio's video preview/editing player, so you can add chapters during your final review pass before publishing.

Is my data private?

Yes. Every timestamp and setting is stored locally in your own browser (chrome.storage.local) — nothing is uploaded to any server we run. There's no tracking or analytics in the extension. Subscriptions are processed entirely by ExtensionPay/Stripe; we never see your card details. Read the full privacy policy.

Why does my first chapter need to start at 0:00?

That's a YouTube requirement, not ours — YouTube only turns a description's timestamp list into clickable chapters if the first entry starts at 0:00 and you have at least three chapters, each at least 10 seconds apart.

Can I use a different hotkey than Alt+Shift+T?

Yes, on the Premium plan — open the toolbar popup, click "change" next to the hotkey, and press whatever combo you want. Free plan uses the default Alt+Shift+T.

Is this affiliated with YouTube or Google?

No. This is an independent, third-party extension built for video editors and creators. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC.
