Frequently asked

Does it work on both Amazon and Etsy?

Yes — the dashboard detects Amazon product pages (/dp/… and /gp/product/…) and Etsy listing pages (/listing/…) automatically and injects itself only there.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything StockSpy reads or that you type into the calculator is stored locally in your own browser. The only network calls are to our billing provider, ExtensionPay, to check subscription status.

How is "margin" calculated?

Net payout = price − (price × platform fee %) − fixed fees − shipping − supplier cost. Margin is net payout divided by price. Fee assumptions are editable per listing.

Can I cancel Premium anytime?

Yes, subscriptions are managed through ExtensionPay's account page, linked from the upgrade flow — cancel whenever you like, no lock-in.

Privacy, in short

  • StockSpy reads publicly visible page content (title, price, availability text) only on the Amazon or Etsy page you're actively viewing.
  • Everything is stored locally in your browser's extension storage — nothing is transmitted to a server we operate.
  • Payment details for Premium are entered directly on ExtensionPay's hosted checkout (processed via Stripe) and never pass through this extension.
  • No remote code — every script ships inside the installed extension package.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
