Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

StockSpy ("the extension," "we," "us") is a Chrome extension that overlays a price, stock-level, and profit-margin dashboard on Amazon and Etsy product pages. This policy describes what data the extension handles and how.

What StockSpy reads

When you have a StockSpy panel open on an Amazon or Etsy product page, the extension reads publicly visible content already on that page in your browser — the product title, price, availability/stock text, and variant option names. This happens only on pages you are actively browsing; StockSpy does not visit pages on your behalf.

Where that data goes

Everything StockSpy reads, or that you type into its profit calculator (supplier cost, shipping fee, fee assumptions, and any product you choose to "track"), is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local), on your own device. We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or has access to this data. It is never sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party by us.

Payments (ExtensionPay / Stripe)

If you choose to upgrade to Premium, StockSpy opens a checkout page hosted at extensionpay.com, operated by ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe to process the payment. Your payment card details are entered directly on that hosted page and are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe — they never pass through StockSpy's code or storage. StockSpy only receives a subscription status flag (paid / not paid) back from ExtensionPay's API so it can unlock Premium features.

What StockSpy does not do

  • It does not collect or transmit your browsing history.
  • It does not collect personally identifiable information, location, health information, or authentication credentials.
  • It does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to any third party.
  • It does not use remote code — all logic ships inside the installed extension package.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling StockSpy from chrome://extensions removes all locally stored data associated with it, including your tracked product list and calculator inputs. If you have an active Premium subscription, uninstalling does not cancel billing — manage or cancel your subscription from the ExtensionPay account page linked in the extension's upgrade flow.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
